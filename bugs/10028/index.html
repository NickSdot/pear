Description:

You describe your indentation style as "BSD/Allman" on the function definition page. In all actuality, placing the opening curly bracket on the proceeding line for functions and classes is used in most of the common indentation styles (K&R, BSD/Allman, BSD/KNF, etc.). Your indentation style would probably be more properly described as K&R (Kernighan and Ritchie) style.

Um... it's not so much as having a "function" indentation style as it is having *one* overall indentation style, or at least basing a standard off one indentation style with minor variations upon said style.

By coincidence, the rules for indentation concerning functions are identical in BSD/Allman and K&R. However, your conditional blocks are indented like K&R. Given this, the indentation style is *more accurately* described as K&R and *not* BSD/Allman. Doing so will avoid confusion stemming from the previously defined standards for the indentation style on control structures conflicting with the standard layed out in BSD/Allman for control structures.
