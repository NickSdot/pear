The Russian Translation of the PEAR Documentation displays incorrectly. For example: http://pear.php.net/manual/ru/

The problem is, the text is KOI8-R encoded, but the encoding sent with the headers is UTF-8.

If you decide to leave the Russian translation in the 8-bit format, I'd ask you to convert it to Windows-1251 charset instead of KOI8-R. Windows-1251 is much more convenient and better supported.
