[2007-01-04 10:10 UTC] mansion at php dot net

The example you based your work upon is wrong.
It should read
$form->registerRule('check_pswd', 'callback', 'is_pswd_ok');
instead of
$form->registerRule('check_pswd', 'callback', 'is_pswd_ok',
$form);
since 'is_pswd_ok' lives in global scope.
