[list=1][*] Item one

[/list]

1. Item one
2. Item two
3. Item three

[/list]

the result of this is:

1.
2. Item one
3. Item two
4. Item three

Expected result:
----------------
[list=1]
[*] Item one
[*] Item two
[*] Item three
[/list]

the result of this is:

1. Item one
2. Item two
