[2006-02-07 15:22 UTC] ritzmo at php dot net

As the version of the package is 0.0.1 and the status is alpha it's quite clear that it would not function properly but in my tests it worked very good. the aim of our package is to provide a framework to parse mediawiki-syntax and not to provide a whole wiki-class as mediawiki does.
we could not use the parser of mediawiki as it is not compatible with text_wiki and works in a very different way.
so - indeed - we are reinventing the wheel in one way but our wheel belongs to a much cooler car ;-)
but if you need a stable and fully functional mediawiki-parser you should stick with the official one until we reach a more stable version.
