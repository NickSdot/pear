[2005-09-27 04:29 UTC] daniel at konczyk dot net

You need to encode the var before you use htmlentities, otherwise, htmlentities encodes the multibyte char wrong.

I've made a change in _http_build_query_wrapper function:
1175 array_push($tmp, key.'='.urlencode($val));

And that fixes the problem. Well, as far as I tested..
I'm using 2.3.0
