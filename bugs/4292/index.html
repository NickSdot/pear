Description:
------------
The sample code for Format::setPattern
http://pear.php.net/manual/en/package.fileformats.spreadsheet-excel-writer.spreadsheet-excel-writer-format.setpattern.php
has a few mistakes.

Could use a small change to these three lines:
$worksheet->write(1, $i, "another pattern", $another_format1);
$worksheet->write(1, $i, "another pattern", $another_format2);
$worksheet->write(1, $i, "another pattern", $another_format3);

I recommend they be changed to:
$worksheet->write(1, $i, "bg=r", $another_format1);
$worksheet->write(2, $i, "fg=r", $another_format2);
$worksheet->write(3, $i, "fg=r,bg=g", $another_format3);

That way, you actually get three lines of output. And you can easily see which examples are which by the bg= and gb= text.
