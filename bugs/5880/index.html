$field = 'æøå';
echo "Name:\t\t\t$field, " . utf8_decode($field) . "\n";
echo "md5:\t\t\t" . md5($field) . "\n";
echo "utf8<->utf8, md5:\t" . md5(utf8_encode(utf8_decode($field))) . "\n";

[2005-11-26 22:17 UTC] brian at matzon dot dk

I am reopening bug since I have identified the cause and the fix (though I am unable to provide a patch file).

Hacking around the encode and decode file, I have successfully made it utf8 aware (as it should be!).

Basically by utf8 encoding and decoding the strings everything works as fine, except we're getting wrong hash'es. The reason for this is because the string 'pieces' is being en/de-coded too, which it shouldn't.

The fix therefor must encode and decode only the correct strings and not any and all strings.
Currently this means that all strings should be handled, except the 'pieces' string. I am unsure about the 'name' in multi file mode since it's listed as a "character string" as opposed to a normal string.
