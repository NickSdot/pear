Line 194 of DB.php v1.4.5 (and other files/versions ?) join(y, x)'s the 'db_fields' option with the parameters in the wrong order.

Reproduce code:
---------------
$this->options['db_fields'] =
join($this->options['db_fields'], ', ');

...should be:

$this->options['db_fields'] =
join(', ', $this->options['db_fields']);
