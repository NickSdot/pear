I've found the problem, it's the \r within the code.

Works fine for me :)

[2005-02-01 17:27 UTC] webmaster at phpmyforum dot de

no it's the normal Windows-like \r\n

you can use this, to fix it:

$string = preg_replace('#\r?\n#', "\n", $string);
